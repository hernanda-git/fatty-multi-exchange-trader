## Verification boundaries

A green unit suite is not proof that Bitget accepted a live order. Offline lifecycle smoke must exercise admission, persisted market/limit legs, waiting fill, native protection readback, restart and source cancellation. Disposable PostgreSQL must exercise migrations, exact baseline exclusions, future unresolved ownership and financial history preservation. Deployment must verify image/source lineage, all operational health checks, authenticated provider inventory, recovery readiness and entry-latch state.

No live canary or historical signal replay is authorized by deployment. The first real future signal remains the live placement/fill validation boundary; do not label queue creation, pending acknowledgement, trigger execution or local protection intent as provider fill/protection proof.

## Remaining operational risks

- Historic financial ownership remains explicitly uncertain after baseline; it is not historical reconciliation or verified-close accounting.
- Telegram delivery is at-least-once across acceptance-before-database-ack crashes. Operator mutation receipts remain at-most-once and can lose execution after a committed receipt and crash; never replay blindly.
- Native plan execution IDs and actual closing fill IDs must be correlated; a trigger firing is not proof of an executed close.
- Unsupported native protection is not safe merely because a fallback row exists. Existing fallback and stream mutation gates remain unchanged; the configured protection path must prove actual enforceability.
- Current empty account reads do not prove that a human or external process will not trade during a controlled recovery. Stop local mutation consumers and avoid external trading throughout apply.
- Model/image semantic accuracy and external reverse-proxy exposure are not established by code inspection.
